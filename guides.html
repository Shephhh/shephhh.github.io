PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO STAY IN THE RUN

Controls

On desktop, use A/D or the left and right movement controls to move sideways. Space jumps over low hazards; use slide when a high barrier blocks the route. Mouse movement controls your view. On touch devices, swipe to move, jump, and slide.

Read the road

Do not chase every coin. First make a safe route through traffic and barriers, then collect the line of gold that remains. Trains and large vehicles need early decisions because their collision area is wider than a small obstacle.

Spend gold with a purpose

Upgrade score and gold for steady long-term value. Jump improvements make dense routes safer. Active abilities are strongest when saved for a difficult sequence, not used as soon as they become available.

Daily missions

Daily missions reward consistent play. Complete an objective, then claim its reward from the mission panel. Claimed rewards are added to your wallet for upgrades.
