WORLD ROUTE

SIX WORLDS. ONE RUN.

Cyber City

Neon roads, traffic, trains, and the quickest opening pace. The city teaches the basic rhythm of avoiding moving obstacles.

Sky

Elevated roads and floating structures make the route feel open. Keep your view forward when bridges and airborne hazards arrive.

Nature

A calmer-looking route with a different visual tempo, broad scenery, and obstacles that reward careful lane positioning.

Lava, Water, and Snow

These elemental worlds add their own road effects and atmosphere: volcanic heat, flowing water routes, and ice-lined snow scenery. They are designed as a continuous journey rather than separate levels.
