ABOUT THE GAME

ONE ROAD, SIX ELEMENTS.

ELEMENTAL RUN is an original first-person 3D endless runner. Its central idea is simple: read the road at speed. Players dodge traffic and hazards, jump, slide, collect gold, and decide when to activate an earned ability.

What makes a run different?

The road uses changing obstacle sequences rather than a fixed course. A safe line through a train may not be the best line through the next road barrier. As a run progresses, the scenery shifts between Cyber City, Sky, Nature, Lava, Water, and Snow, while the game keeps the first-person viewpoint and direct controls consistent.

How the browser edition is maintained

The browser edition is designed to be played without a launcher. Updates focus on loading reliability, clear obstacle visibility, responsive controls, and graphics choices that can scale from capable desktop hardware to smaller mobile devices. Development notes are recorded on the updates page.
